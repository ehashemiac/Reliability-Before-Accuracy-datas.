# Amendment Log v4 — 2026-09-09

## Amendment 3 — Capacity-matched primary pool and primary criteria
Reason: mixed-capacity dishwashers confounded per-cycle energy/water with product size.
Old rule: mixed capacity; warranty could act as a primary criterion.
New rule: capacity fixed at 14 place settings; primary criteria fixed to energy/cycle, water/cycle, noise, and programme duration.
Consequence: the empirical estimand is now like-for-like 14-place dishwasher procurement.

## Amendment 4 — Provenance tiering and pool expansion
Reason: the manual official-EPREL route yielded enough records to construct a larger pool, but some public category-index rows did not expose registration numbers in the extracted text.
New rule: use 24 individual-page records plus 20 official-category-index records, with explicit provenance tiers and transparent internal IDs for index-backed rows.
Consequence: main pool is 44 real records; no third-party mirror is in the frozen primary pool.

## Amendment 5 — Primary criterion simplification
Reason: warranty was not consistently available with equivalent provenance across the complete frozen pool. Price and repairability were structurally absent/inconsistent for the selected category.
New rule: primary benchmark uses energy/cycle, water/cycle, noise, and programme duration only.
Consequence: all main tasks use complete four-field records, eliminating criterion-level missingness in the base pool.
