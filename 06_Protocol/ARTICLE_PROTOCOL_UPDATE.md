# Article protocol update

## Empirical framing
The primary empirical context is sustainable and resilient procurement of household dishwashers, not a direct measurement of circularity. DPP/ESPR are used as the digital-product-information and regulatory context.

## Frozen primary data
44 real EPREL dishwasher product/model records, all rated capacity = 14 place settings. 24 are supported by individual official EPREL product pages; 20 by the official EPREL category index.

## Primary criteria
- energy per eco cycle (cost)
- water per eco cycle (cost)
- airborne noise (cost)
- eco-programme duration (cost)

## Main experiment
40 decision tasks × 4 architecture arms × 2 fixed Nemotron models = 320 experimental conditions.

## API request count
A, B and C use one model request each; D uses parser + audit = two. Therefore 400 model requests are expected before transient retries.

## No-result rule
No empirical result may be written into the manuscript until `scored_main_v7.csv` and QC are valid and the statistical plan is satisfied.
