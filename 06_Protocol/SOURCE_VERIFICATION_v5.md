# Source verification v5

The official EPREL dishwasher category page is the primary source for the 20 index-backed records and explicitly identifies Regulation (EU) 2019/2017 while displaying product-level rated capacity, energy per 100 cycles, water per cycle, programme duration and noise.

Individual official product pages directly verified in the current research cycle include Bosch SBT4ECX33E (EPREL 2088104), Siemens SN63EX27CE (2068965), Beko BDIN16440 (2253240), Beko KBDIN184E0Q (581126), Bosch SMV4EVX00E (1319322), plus the previously audited individual-page records in the frozen pool.

All frozen primary records are capacity 14. The dataset transformation `energy_per_cycle_kwh = energy_per_100_cycles_kwh / 100` is deterministic where the official index displays the per-100-cycles value. Programme duration is converted from h:min to integer minutes.

Citation targets for manuscript: official EPREL category page and the individual product pages should be cited where exact product-level examples are discussed.
